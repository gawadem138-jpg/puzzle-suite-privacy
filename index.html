Privacy Policy for Puzzle Suite

Effective Date: November 18, 2026

libe ("we", "our", or "us") developed Puzzle Suite (com.libe.puzzlesuite) as a free, offline-playable, ad-supported mobile puzzle collection. This Privacy Policy explains how information is handled when you use the app.

1. No Account & Local On-Device Storage

Puzzle Suite (Sudoku, Minesweeper, Tango, and Queens) runs completely offline without user accounts, registration, or sign-in. We do not collect, store, or transmit your name, email address, or personal profile information on our own servers.

The following gameplay data is stored strictly locally on your device using Android Jetpack DataStore:

  • Personal Leaderboard & Scores: Local completion times, difficulty levels, Pure vs. Assisted run flags, and win history.
  • Daily Streaks: Independent per-game streak counters (current streak, best streak, and last completed date).
  • Saved Game Progress & Preferences: In-progress puzzle boards for resuming games, theme selection (White/Dark), haptics, and gameplay assist toggles.

2. Local Notifications

With your permission (requested on Android 13 and higher), Puzzle Suite schedules optional, on-device daily streak reminders and streak milestone notifications using Android WorkManager. All notification checks run locally on your device without external push servers. You can turn off streak reminders at any time via Settings → Streak Reminders or your device notification settings.

3. Third-Party Advertising (Google AdMob)

Puzzle Suite uses Google AdMob to display banner, interstitial, and optional rewarded-hint advertisements when an internet connection is available. AdMob is an independent third-party service provided by Google LLC and may collect and process:

  • Android Advertising ID or similar device/app instance identifiers.
  • IP address (which may be used to estimate general region/location).
  • Basic device information and ad interaction/diagnostic telemetry for ad delivery, frequency capping, fraud prevention, and performance measurement.

For more information on how Google uses data from apps that use Google services, please review Google's Privacy & Terms.

4. Data Security, Retention & Deletion

Any network traffic used by Google AdMob to load and measure advertisements is encrypted in transit using HTTPS/TLS. We do not sell your personal information.

Because all Puzzle Suite scores, streaks, saved games, and settings remain exclusively on your device, you have complete control over your data:

  • In-App Reset: Permanently erase all locally stored scores, streaks, saved boards, and preferences at any time via Settings → Clear Scores & Data.
  • Uninstall: Uninstalling Puzzle Suite removes all locally stored app data from your device.

5. Children's Privacy

Puzzle Suite is intended for general audiences aged 13 and older and does not knowingly collect personally identifiable information from children under 13. If you believe a child has provided personal information in connection with our app, please contact us so we can assist promptly.

6. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time to reflect app updates or legal requirements. Any changes will be posted on this page with an updated effective date.
